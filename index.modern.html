Snap Research NYC

Research Engineer building human-centered generative systems.

I'm a staff research engineer at Snap Research NYC. My research interests focus on 3D human animation generation from various signals (text, music, audio, video), human-centered video generation (audio-conditioned lip-synced video, background generation, video-audio joint generation), 3D avatar/animal reconstruction and animation, and human-centered sensing.
